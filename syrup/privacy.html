Privacy Policy — Syrup

Effective: October 14, 2026

Syrup is a mobile SSH client for Android. This policy describes how Syrup handles your information.

Data stored on your device

To function as an SSH client, Syrup stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Server configurations you enter: display names, hostnames, ports, usernames, and authentication preferences.
  • Credentials: SSH private keys and passwords you choose to save, encrypted with Android Keystore-backed storage.
  • SSH host key fingerprints for servers you connect to, used to warn you if a server's identity changes.
  • Session metadata: session labels, connection state, and timestamps.

What Syrup does not do

  • Syrup has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics.
  • Your data is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • Syrup contains no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The terminal interface is bundled with the app and loads no remote content.

Network connections

Syrup connects only to the SSH servers you configure. It requests Internet access for this purpose, and network-state access to detect connectivity changes. No other app permissions are used.

Device backups

If you have Android's backup feature enabled, Android itself may back up app data to your Google account. This is handled entirely by the Android operating system, not by Syrup, and the developer has no access to it.

Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time: remove individual servers or keys inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

Syrup is a general-purpose utility not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
